Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

At MomCalc, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our pregnancy tracking tools and website.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide you with the best possible experience:

  • Pregnancy Data: Due dates, cycle lengths, and tracking data (like kick counts) are stored locally on your device using localStorage. We do not transmit this personal health data to our servers.
  • Usage Data: Basic analytics (like page views and feature usage) to help us improve the platform.
  • Location Data: Used strictly temporarily when you use the "Doctor Finder" feature to show clinics near you.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Calculate accurate due dates and pregnancy milestones.
  • Provide local clinic recommendations.
  • Improve our platform's functionality and user experience.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your personal pregnancy and health data remains on your device. We use modern web storage APIs (localStorage) meaning your sensitive information never leaves your phone or computer unless you explicitly choose to export or share it.

4. Third-Party Services

We use OpenStreetMap for the Doctor Finder feature and standard analytics tools. These third parties are bound by their own privacy policies and do not have access to your personal pregnancy data.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@momcalc.com.
