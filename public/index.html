🥗 Nutrition Guide

Essential vitamins, minerals, and food guidance for a healthy pregnancy

💊
Folic Acid
400–600 mcg/day
Critical for neural tube development. Start before conception and continue through the 1st trimester.
🩸
Iron
27 mg/day
Supports increased blood volume and prevents anemia. Pair with Vitamin C for better absorption.
🦴
Calcium
1,000 mg/day
Essential for baby's bones, teeth, heart, muscles, and nerves. Found in dairy and leafy greens.
🐟
DHA / Omega-3
200–300 mg/day
Vital for baby's brain and eye development. Found in fatty fish, flaxseed, and prenatal supplements.
☀️
Vitamin D
600 IU/day
Works with calcium for bone health. Many pregnant women are deficient — check with your doctor.
🥑
Choline
450 mg/day
Supports baby's brain development and helps prevent neural tube defects, often underrated.

🍱 Foods Guide

What to eat and what to avoid during pregnancy

✅ Best Foods to Eat
  • Leafy greens (spinach, kale)
  • Salmon & sardines — DHA
  • Eggs — choline, protein
  • Avocado — healthy fats
  • Berries — antioxidants
  • Greek yogurt — calcium
  • Lentils & beans — iron, fiber
❌ Foods to Avoid
  • Raw / undercooked meat
  • High-mercury fish
  • Unpasteurized dairy
  • Alcohol — AVOID
  • High-caffeine drinks
🏥 Medical Guide

Important health conditions, warning signs, and when to call your doctor

🚨 Call 911 Immediately If…
  • 🔴Heavy vaginal bleeding
  • 🔴Severe abdominal or pelvic pain
  • 🔴Sudden severe headache
  • 🔴Chest pain or difficulty breathing
⚠️ Call Your Doctor Today If…
  • 🟡Fever over 38°C (100.4°F)
  • 🟡Sudden swelling of face or hands
  • 🟡Reduced fetal movement
  • 🟡Vaginal bleeding or unusual discharge
🩺 Preeclampsia Warning Signs
  • ⚡BP reading ≥ 140/90
  • ⚡Sudden severe headache
  • ⚡Visual disturbances (spots/lights)
  • ⚡Sudden significant face/hand swelling
💊 Safe Medications Guide
  • ✅Acetaminophen (Tylenol)
  • ✅Prenatal vitamins with DHA
  • ❌Ibuprofen (Advil) — AVOID after 20 wks
  • ⚠️Consult doctor before any medication
