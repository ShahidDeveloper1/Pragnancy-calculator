Woche 13: Hello, Second Trimester!

Zweites Trimester

Größe: Cabbage

Week 13 is a period of remarkable transformation. Your baby is now focusing all their energy on gaining weight and refining the complex systems that will sustain them. Every day, their brain is forming new connections, and their body is becoming more proportioned and ready for the world.
