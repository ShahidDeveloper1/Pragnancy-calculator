أسبوع 38: Full Term Perfection

الثالث الثلث

الحجم: Winter Melon

Week 38 is a period of remarkable transformation. Your baby is now focusing all their energy on gaining weight and refining the complex systems that will sustain them. Every day, their brain is forming new connections, and their body is becoming more proportioned and ready for the world.

الإنجازات

  • ✦ Nervous system maturity reaches new heights.
  • ✦ Building up subcutaneous fat for temperature regulation.
  • ✦ Strengthening the suck-swallow-breathe coordination.
  • ✦ Developing unique sleep and wake patterns you might start to notice.

نصيحة الرعاية
