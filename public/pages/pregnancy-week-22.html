Pregnancy Week 22: Touch Sensitivity

Second Trimester

Baby is the size of a Papaya — Weight: 2.2kg, Length: 27cm

Week 22 marks a significant phase in your baby's development. At this stage, growth is the primary focus as all systems continue to mature and prepare for life outside the womb.

Baby Development at Week 22

  • ✅ Rapid organ maturation
  • ✅ Weight gain for temperature control
  • ✅ Nervous system refinement
  • ✅ Refining motor skills

Common Symptoms at Week 22

Fatigue
Backache
Increased appetite

Expert Tips for Week 22

💡 Ensure you are staying active with gentle exercises like walking or prenatal yoga.

Frequently Asked Questions

How big is my baby at 22 weeks?

At week 22, your baby is approximately the size of a Papaya, measuring about 27cm in length and weighing around 2.2kg.

What trimester is week 22?

Week 22 falls in the Second Trimester of pregnancy. The second trimester covers weeks 13-26.

What symptoms should I expect at week 22?

Common symptoms at week 22 include: Fatigue, Backache, Increased appetite. Every pregnancy is different — consult your doctor if symptoms concern you.
