Can Pregnant Women Eat Soft Cheese?

⚠️ Caution Advised

Unpasteurized soft cheeses like Brie, Camembert, and Feta can harbor Listeria. Only consume soft cheeses if they are clearly labeled as 'made with pasteurized milk.'

Why to Avoid During Pregnancy

Unpasteurized soft cheeses like Brie, Camembert, and Feta can harbor Listeria. Only consume soft cheeses if they are clearly labeled as 'made with pasteurized milk.'

Safe alternatives: Hard cheeses (Cheddar, Parmesan), or pasteurized versions of soft cheese.
